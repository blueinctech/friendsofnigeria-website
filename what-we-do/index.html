What we do

Five areas of work, one purpose: helping Nigerian professionals in Europe thrive and connecting European companies and investors with opportunities in Nigeria.

  1. 01Diaspora engagement
  2. 02Social support and integration
  3. 03Investment promotion
  4. 04Training and professional development
  5. 05Bilateral engagements

Diaspora engagement

Bridging communities for nation-building

We engage the Nigerian diaspora community on developmental projects, events and initiatives, and partner with diaspora organisations, NGOs and international bodies to advocate for a stronger inclusion of the diaspora in nation-building.

Learn more about diaspora engagement

Social support and integration

Overcoming barriers for a fulfilling educational experience

Our social integration and support programme helps new and returning students network, learn, and acquire the information they need to make the most of their stay in Europe.

Learn more about social support and integration

Investment promotion

Catalysing growth through strategic business engagement

Business breakfast meetings, bespoke trade missions and market matchmaking that help French and European companies understand the Nigerian business environment and access the right partnerships.

Learn more about investment promotion

Training and professional development

Enhancing skills for business and professional excellence

Capacity development sessions for our members, and in-house training for French and European companies that want to understand investing and doing business in Nigeria.

Learn more about training and professional development

Bilateral engagements

Elevating partnerships for mutual prosperity

We take part in federal-level events and meetings that reinforce ties between Europe and Nigeria, and organise investment missions to Paris for Nigerian state governments.

Learn more about bilateral engagements

Be part of a network creating opportunities between Nigeria and Europe.

Join Nigerian professionals and European companies building partnerships, sharing expertise and opening doors on both continents.

Friends of Nigeria (FON) is a non-profit that facilitates access for Nigerian professionals in Europe to business and professional development opportunities, and promotes Nigeria's attractiveness to European companies and investors.
