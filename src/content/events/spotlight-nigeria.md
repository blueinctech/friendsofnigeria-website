The Spotlight Nigeria Conference and Exhibition is an annual event that offers a platform to private sector companies and investors from France and Nigeria to collaborate, exchange ideas, and partner together for mutual benefit.

The event offers participants an opportunity to build business contacts, sign deals, build strategies, and increase their knowledge of the opportunities that currently exist for investment across Nigeria, across key sectors of the economy and key state governments. Each year it focuses on key sectors of the Nigerian economy and attracts participation from the public and private sectors of Nigeria to meet with counterparts from France and Europe at large.

From its maiden edition in 2014, Spotlight Nigeria has become the foremost event in France for European companies, business leaders and investors interested in investment and business opportunities in Nigeria.
