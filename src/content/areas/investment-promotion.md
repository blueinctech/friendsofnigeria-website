We organise business breakfast meetings and coordinate bespoke trade missions which provide an opportunity for participating companies and individuals to understand the Nigerian business environment better and access the right partnerships and opportunities.

We also match-make French and European member companies with the right advisory and market research necessary to introduce their products and services into the Nigerian market.

Our investment promotion efforts are centred on creating platforms for meaningful dialogue and partnership. These gatherings are designed to pave the way for fruitful partnerships and opportunities, and through them we aim to catalyse sustainable economic growth and development.
