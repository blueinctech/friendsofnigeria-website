An analysis of several research papers and publications reveals that, among the top challenges facing international students, three are the most overarching: language barriers, coping with cultural differences, and the lack of a support network upon arrival. Our social integration and support programme helps new and returning students network, learn, and acquire the information necessary to maximise their stay in Europe.

We have designed the programme to facilitate a smooth transition for new and returning students, enabling them to fully embrace their educational journey in France. From learning the local language to finding accommodation and accessing vital resources, our network serves as a beacon of support.

We empower students to network, learn, and gather the information they need, so that they not only succeed academically but also thrive socially and culturally.
