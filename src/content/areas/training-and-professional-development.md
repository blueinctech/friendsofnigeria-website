We organise training and capacity development sessions for our members. These trainings focus on business and professional subjects and are targeted at our members.

We also organise in-house training for French and European companies who seek to better understand investing in Nigeria, its business culture, and the Nigerian business environment.

Our sessions are crafted to meet the evolving needs of our members, from in-house sessions for companies eager to navigate the Nigerian business climate to workshops focused on members' growth and development. Our approach is to equip participants with the knowledge and skills required to thrive in their fields, so they are well prepared to tackle the challenges and seize the opportunities of investing and operating in Nigeria.
