We actively participate, at federal level, in events and meetings that reinforce bilateral engagement between Europe and Nigeria.

We also recognise the value and impact of foreign partnership and investment at state level in Nigeria, and organise exclusive investment business missions to Paris for Nigerian state governments to meet and pitch investment opportunities within their states to selected French stakeholders, public and private.

These efforts reinforce bilateral ties and spotlight Nigeria's potential as fertile ground for investment, driving economic growth and development.
