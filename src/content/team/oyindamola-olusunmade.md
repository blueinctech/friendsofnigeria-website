Oyindamola Olusunmade is a Financial services consultant currently working at Altran, a global innovation and engineering consulting firm. She has almost four years work experience in France, Luxembourg, and Nigeria with multinational companies across diverse sectors including Fintech, Investment Banking, Aerospace and Asset Management. Specifically, Oyindamola’s previous clients and employers include corporations such as Airbus, PayPal, AXA Investment Managers and Société Générale Corporate and Investment Bank. While serving these organisations, her cognate work experience cut across a vast range of business concerns to include Finance, Risk Management, Regulatory Due Diligence and Compliance.

Oyindamola obtained her bachelor’s degree in Economics from Caleb University in Nigeria and is a holder of a Masters of Finance degree from Grenoble Ecole de Management in France. She has also passed Level I of the CFA Program.

Oyindamola enjoys travelling, experiencing different cultures through food, arts and languages. She speaks French, Yoruba and English. When she is not at work, she is exploring the world and appreciating the diversity in its people and cultures.
