Ifeanyi Nwadishi is a result driven IT Professional with extensive experience in IT Governance and Operations.

Having acquired over 10 years of experience in Information Technology in different business sectors such as Training and development for youths in ways that build their innovative and creative capacities, Insurance, Manufacturing, Agriculture, Software development etc.

Ifeanyi has developed and worked on projects with organizations like MindAfrica Leadership Initiative on IT empowerment and youth development for Teenagers and youths in underserved communities in South-South Region of Nigeria.

His passion for Innovation and love for Electronics prompt him to developing a Remote control for Fans(ventilators) in Nigeria. This idea was borne out of his passion for giving back to the community after an encounter with some physically challenged group of people in Nigeria. This initiative was what led to his being a board member and one of the creative minds of a Startup in Nigeria (Royallabs Technologies) that specialized in providing Electrical and Electronics solutions such as the Auto-Hydro gauge, Solar panels etc. He is also a founding member of a Tech Start-up SMAT(See my Africa Tech) in Nigeria where they develop software solutions and handle various IT related Projects.

Presently, Ifeanyi works as an Infrastructure Analyst with one of the biggest software development company in the World (ADOBE).

He enjoys playing football, Movies, Surfing the web for Latest technologies, creative thinking for new solutions, travelling with friends and acquiring new skills.

He holds a Bachelor’s of Science in Computer Science and a Master’s degree in Information System Management from EPITA (Paris). He also has various IT certifications from Cisco, Microsoft and Comptia.
