Bukky Kadiri is a Creative Graphic designer, who transforms ideas into visually compelling designs that communicate brand messages and captivate audiences. She thrives in creating clean, modern, and dynamic visuals that not only look great but deliver measurable impact across digital and print platforms.

By combining her expertise in Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, XD) with a deep understanding of UI/UX principles and creative problem-solving, I transform ideas into engaging designs that resonate with target audiences.

She holds a Bachelor’s degree in Library and Information Science, which has greatly contributed to my ability to organize and manage visual assets, ensuring they are accessible and efficient for use in content creation.

Her background in information science also honed her skills in research and data analysis, which she leverages to understand market trends and user behavior, ensuring my designs are not only creative but also strategically effective.

Outside of Work Bukky enjoys singing, reading, cooking and exploring new places.
