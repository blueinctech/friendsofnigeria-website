Serge has accumulated a wide experience as a consultant, as an entrepreneur and as a senior manager within large companies. He started his career at the Boston Consulting Group where was elected Vice President and Director in 1985. He initiated an entrepreneurial venture in 1991 and launched a retail service chain. After the sale of the business, he then joined Capgemini in 2001 where he held several senior management positions. In 2010, He decided to join OC&C a leading consulting firm where he was elected partner.

In 2015 he founded SCB-SUBSAHARA with a team of recognized high level experts. The company is dedicated to assisting corporations in their global expansion and offers a full range of services from designing international strategic plans to monitoring sales generation programs.

Serge is recognized as a specialist of growth issues and international development. In this area, he has authored several books: “Quand les RH construisent la croissance” (2005), “Notre avenir depend d’eux” (2010) and lately, together with several CEO’s of large groups, he contributed to a book called, “La France et ses Multinationales”.

He has delivered numerous speeches and published a number of articles on his preferred themes.

Serge Blanchard is a graduate of Ecole Centrale de Paris and obtained an MBA at the Wharton School of Finance, University of Pennsylvania
