Victory Ayele is a communications and development professional with over 8 years of experience driving strategic communications, advocacy, and media engagement across corporate, humanitarian, and development sectors. His work spans agriculture, food systems, climate resilience, and livelihoods, where he has consistently elevated the visibility and impact of organisations through evidence-based storytelling, public relations, and thought leadership.

Victory currently serves as the Operations and Communications Manager (Nigeria) for Friends of Nigeria Europe (FoN. In this role, he coordinates operations and communication strategies in Nigeria, working closely with the leadership team in Europe to implement FoN’s flagship initiatives such as the Spotlight Nigeria Business & Investment Forum (Paris) and the Nigerians in Diaspora Awards (NIDA). These events have become premier platforms that connect policymakers, investors, and innovators from Africa and Europe to celebrate excellence, attract diaspora investments, and strengthen Nigeria’s global image.

Victory is known for his creativity and systems-thinking approach, merging strategic communication with social impact. He has designed and managed campaigns that highlight inclusive growth, gender equality, and resilience among smallholder farmers and displaced populations, working across diverse contexts and international donor frameworks.

An advocate for ethical storytelling and youth empowerment, Victory is also the founder of Jasi Cleaning Services, an eco-friendly cleaning and waste management enterprise based in Abuja, focused on sustainability, job creation, and environmental protection.

Victory is passionate about Africa’s transformation narrative. He continues to inspire through his writing and creative storytelling.
