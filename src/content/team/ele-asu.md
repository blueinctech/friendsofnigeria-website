Élé Asu is a Nigerian-born communications professional with a background in broadcast journalism.

She is a seasoned interviewer, well versed in decrypting complex issues for a broad audience with a mix of friendly banter and sharp attention to detail. She hosts with ease in English (her native language) and in French.

Élé was a news anchor and talk-show host for over a decade on pay tv network Canal+. Her achievements include co-hosting the morning show “La Matinale” in France from 2007 to 2012, and launching the flagship business program [Réussite], leading brand in 20+ African countries from 2014 to 2017.
