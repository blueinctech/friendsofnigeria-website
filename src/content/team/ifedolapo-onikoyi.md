Princess Ifèdolapo Onikoyi, is a multidisciplinary creative force whose journey bridges fashion and contemporary art. With a Bachelor in Business Administration and Marketing from the University of Greenwich, her career began in the fast-paced worlds of fashion and event management when she started “Crystal Deroche Agency”.The agency provides various services from styling photography shoots to directing artistic fashion editorials and shows during London, Paris and Lagos Fashion Weeks, Princess Ifèdolapo honed her expertise in form, texture, and color, gradually evolving her focus to wider artistic expressions.

Her love for visual arts was sparked at the age of 12 when she discovered Matisse, whose vibrant use of colors and forms mesmerized her. This early passion led her to explore art inspired by her Yoruba heritage and the works of renowned African artists like Ben Enwonwu, Yinka Shonibare, and Twin Seven Seven. This fusion of cultural influences became the backbone of her unique artistic signature, blending traditional and contemporary themes in both her fashion and art projects.

In 2020, she founded Artfrofest, an art agency dedicated to promoting emerging and established from Africa and its diaspora. Princess Ifèdolapo curates exhibitions, manages events, and leads Art and Cultural projects, fostering intercultural artistic exchanges. The agency serves as a platform for artists to showcase their work globally, offering mentorship, support, and opportunities to enrich the dialogue around African art, as well as socially engaged artistic endeavors.
