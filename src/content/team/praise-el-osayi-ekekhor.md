Praise-El Okoro is a dynamic and globally minded professional with four years of experience in the maritime industry. As a Vessel Optimization Analyst at CMA CGM, she plays a vital role in ensuring the smooth flow of cargo across international trade routes — transforming data and strategy into real-world impact.

Her career journey has taken her from sales and customer success roles in Paris to the heart of global shipping operations, giving her a unique perspective that blends commercial acumen, analytical expertise, and operational excellence. Praise-El thrives at the intersection of people, performance, and purpose — driven by a passion for efficiency, collaboration, and continuous growth.

She holds a BSc in Human Resources Management from Covenant University, Nigeria, and an MSc in Business Analytics and Strategic Management from the Alliance Manchester Business School. Fluent in English and French, Praise-El brings a truly international outlook and a deep appreciation for diversity and innovation in business.

Whether she’s optimizing vessel operations or inspiring the next generation of global professionals, Praise-El is committed to making a difference — connecting insights to action and turning challenges into opportunities for progress.
