Dr. Oluwatoyin F. Sanni is a distinguished Nigerian lawyer, investment banker, and financial strategist, currently serving as Executive Vice-Chair of Emerging Africa Group. She previously led United Capital Plc as Group CEO, and earlier headed United Capital Trustees.

With a foundational education in law and further executive training at Harvard Business School, IESE and Lagos Business School, Dr. Sanni combines legal acumen with financial leadership. She is also a published author, notably Riding the Eagle – A Guide to Investing in Nigeria.

She is a founder of Women in Finance Nigeria, a former board director in leading corporate institutions, and a 2017 All Africa Business Woman of the Year.

Dr. Sanni is widely recognized for her advocacy for women’s economic empowerment and her contributions to sustainable investment across Africa.
