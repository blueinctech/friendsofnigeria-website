Angel is a Nigerian writer, performing poet, and visual storyteller. She is the author of “The Days of Silence” (MASOBE 2021) and “SUNDANCE” (a collection of poems & notes. With a passion for amplifying unheard voices and shedding light on sensitive themes, she seeks to inspire change and foster meaningful dialogue through impactful storytelling.

In 2021, she launched “Poetry and Tea” event, a creative safe space for poetry lovers and creatives to connect and heal through poetry and tea. With a background in Psychology, mental health and well-being are common themes explored in her works.

Angel has worked with the Asmara Addis Literary Festival in Exile (AALFIE) Belgium, and facilitated a class at the Creative Writing Academy for Refugees and asylum seekers in Brussels. Her essays have appeared in AYO magazine and Afreada.
