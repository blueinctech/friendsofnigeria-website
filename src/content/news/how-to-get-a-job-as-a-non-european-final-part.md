## Useful Tips on Career Development

In this final post we will be sharing some skill development resources to support your career as a non-EU citizen. As much as obtaining a master’s degree is very important, acquiring additional skills always gives you an advantage and could be your gateway to a successful job interview.

Please find [**HERE**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1RthYoU3CLKiGdC61vPq6wzh_84qiAYjH/view?usp=sharing) a well detailed excel document that contains every relevant resource you need to develop new or learn new skills and to build an outstanding resume. In the first excel sheet page you will find:

- All free learning resources and free courses on platforms like Ivy League, Harvard, Pluralsight, Udemy and many more

In the second sheet of the document you will find resume and career support resources like;

- Links to resume builders
- Career advice and tips to stand out.

We hope this mini-series has been helpful to you and wish you all the best with finding your next job in Europe!

Good luck.
