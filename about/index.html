About Friends of Nigeria

Friends of Nigeria (FON) is a non-profit involved in facilitating the access of Nigerian professionals in Europe to business and professional development opportunities and promoting Nigeria's attractiveness to European companies and investors.

Meet the team Contact us

Our vision

To be at the forefront of promoting Nigeria in Europe and connecting professionals and businesses to opportunities in Nigeria.

Our mission, in two parts

Our mission guides and inspires every decision we make.

  1. 01

    Building networks

    We are building the largest network of Nigerian professionals in Europe and facilitating their access to entrepreneurship and career opportunities, so they can contribute their quota towards the economic development of Nigeria.

  2. 02

    Mutual partnerships

    We promote economic and commercial relations between France and Nigeria and facilitate mutual partnerships and collaborations between European enterprises and Nigerian enterprises.

Our values

To achieve our goals and aspirations, we strive to abide by these core values and principles.

  • 01

    Synergy

    Our actions are deeply rooted in teamwork, and this runs across our activities and operations. We believe collaboration is the bedrock of every great achievement.

  • 02

    Equity

    Our actions are governed by meritocracy and fairness. FON is not affiliated with any religious, political or tribal propaganda. We treat all our members equally.

  • 03

    Excellence

    As a professional organisation, we work with individuals at the forefront of their careers and industries, and carry out our activities with a dedication to excellence.

  • 04

    Integrity

    We operate at the highest level of transparency and openness with our members, partners and team. Integrity runs through our organisational culture.

Where we work

A network of chapters across Europe, with a liaison office in Nigeria.

  • France

    Our headquarters in Paris, home to Spotlight Nigeria and NIDA, with business, professional and governance committees.

  • Germany

    A country chapter connecting Nigerian professionals and communities across Germany.

  • Belgium & Luxembourg

    A chapter serving Nigerian professionals and partners in Belgium and Luxembourg.

  • Nigeria

    A liaison office coordinating operations and communications with partners in Nigeria.

Head office: 229 rue Saint-Honoré, 75001 Paris, France
